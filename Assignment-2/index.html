Young Lady is bright, pretty and easy to love. Citrus, mandarin and peony open fresh and sparkling, rose and osmanthus bring a soft floral heart, and sandalwood, patchouli and a hint of pink pepper give it a warm, grown-up finish.

Playful but never childish. Made for feeling put-together on an ordinary Tuesday, not just on special occasions.

Top Notes: Citrus, Peony, Mandarin
Heart Notes: Rose, Osmanthus
Base Notes: Patchouli, Pink Pepper, Sandalwood

Best for: Office, daytime, everyday wear

For fans of light, fresh designer florals.

Delivery & Payment
